A clear note about this demo

Privacy Policy

This frontend-only website stores demonstration data on the device and browser where it is used.

This is placeholder privacy information for a local prototype, not legal advice or a production privacy policy.

Information stored in your browser

When you use the demonstration, account details, submitted story text, compressed selected photographs, saved story IDs and newsletter addresses may be saved in your browser's localStorage. This information is not sent to a server by this project.

Storage and security

Browser storage is local to the browser profile and may be visible to someone with access to that device. It is not encrypted or suitable for real account credentials or sensitive information. Do not use real passwords or submit sensitive data in this prototype.

External resources

Some sample photography and fonts are loaded from third-party services. Those services may receive normal connection data when your browser requests their resources.

Future production version

Before public launch, replace this notice with a reviewed policy describing the actual backend, retention practices, account security, moderation, analytics and user rights.

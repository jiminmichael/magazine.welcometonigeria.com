Notes for the road

Travel Well, Travel Thoughtfully

A useful starting point for travelling around Nigeria. Local conditions change, so pair these general notes with up-to-date advice from people who know the place.

01 / Before you go

Plan enough, leave room

Check current local guidance, opening times and access before setting out. Share your broad plans with someone you trust, then leave space for an unexpected stop or recommendation.

02 / Getting around

Build in travel time

Routes and journey times vary. Confirm transport options locally, allow generous buffers and keep important addresses and contact details available offline.

03 / Where to stay

Ask specific questions

Before confirming accommodation, check location, arrival arrangements, transport nearby and what is included. Recent local recommendations can be especially helpful.

04 / Personal safety

Stay aware and informed

Follow current official and local advice for your route. Keep valuables discreet, use trusted transport, and let a companion know when plans change.

05 / What to pack

Pack for your actual route

Comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate layers, water, essential medication, sun protection and a charged phone are useful starting points. Needs differ by season and region.

06 / Spending

Keep a flexible budget

Plan for transport, meals, entry fees and a little extra for delays. Ask about current prices before you commit, and keep a backup payment option where practical.

07 / Across state lines

Check the journey, not just the map

Long-distance travel needs extra planning. Verify departure points, schedules and route conditions close to travel day, and avoid relying on a single connection.

08 / With respect

Be a considerate guest

Learn a greeting where you can, dress with local context in mind and ask permission before photographing people. Listen when residents explain what matters to them.

Advice gets better when it is shared.

Have a practical tip grounded in your own trip? Include it when you share your story. Personal experience helps other travellers prepare, while local conditions should always be checked again before a journey.

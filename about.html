Real Stories. Real People. Real Nigeria.

Welcome to Nigeria Travel Magazine is a place for travellers to share what they experienced, in their own words and through their own photographs.

Our Mission

We want to help people discover Nigeria through first-hand travel experiences. The most meaningful guide to a place is often someone who has just been there: what they noticed, who they met and what they would tell the next traveller.

Why We Created This Magazine

Travel stories deserve more than a list of places. They hold the details that make a journey human: a conversation, a surprise, a change of plan. This magazine gives those experiences a home and makes it easier to explore Nigeria through many points of view.

Our Community

Our contributors are the heart of the magazine. We welcome honest, respectful stories and original photographs from people travelling in every state and the Federal Capital Territory. Submitted work is marked for review before it can become published content.

Share Your Experience

Tell us about a journey that stayed with you. A weekend close to home or a longer trip across state lines can help someone else see a place differently. Share your story and add your point of view to the collection.
